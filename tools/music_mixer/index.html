Instrument balance

Adjustments in dB

Volume changes the part’s level. Warmth shapes its body around 300 Hz; presence shapes definition around 2.5 kHz. Solo is for listening only.

Your changes are saved locally for each track and sound. Export applies the same filters to all aligned parts and lowers the whole mix if needed to prevent sample clipping. Live preview has a safety compressor for large boosts.

The automatic pass uses active phrases from original-sample approximations as its reference, with at most ±6 dB of level correction and ±2 dB of EQ. It preserves musical differences; it does not force every instrument onto one curve. Listening still decides the final balance.
