# Showcase recordings

## Completed — September 21, 2026

The full demo refresh replaces the September 11 GIFs/audio and older verification
stills. All published gameplay media now comes from revision `f3adfad`.

- Sixteen real-time clips cover the portable Lab, hints, settings, two soundtrack
  scenes, memory-card loading, three battle pose rates, map travel/guards, cards,
  gym navigation and the title screen.
- Four selectable 4:3/16:9 pairs cover title, Central Park, battle and Card Album.
- Clips use posters and native playback controls. Action captions accompany the
  longer clips. Nothing autoplays; starting another clip pauses the previous one.
- The soundtrack offers area selection and palette chapters. Recording gain is
  unchanged across palettes and no post EQ is applied.
- Source/capture details and limitations are in [MEDIA.md](MEDIA.md).
